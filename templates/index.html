Why

While you're costing the job, a competitor just cut 12%.

A small distributor carries hundreds of SKUs and has nobody walking competitor price lists every day. The data is public — the habit of checking it is missing.

  • Competitor prices get checked by hand — or not at all.
  • You hear about a price cut from the customer who already compared.
  • The deal goes to whoever is 8% cheaper.

“The data is public. What's missing is the habit of looking at it every day.”

From the product brief

How it works

Three quiet steps, once a day.

  1. Step 01

    Add products and links

    Start with five products and three competitors. Each site gets a profile that says where the price lives on the page.

  2. Step 02

    A snapshot a day

    Collection runs on saved demo pages. A live site plugs in as a separate adapter once the mechanics work.

  3. Step 03

    See the move, get a signal

    The panel shows change since the last snapshot and the market spread. Cross the threshold — an alert and a daily digest follow.

Features

What's inside
