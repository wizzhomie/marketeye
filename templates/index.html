Why

While you're costing the job,
a competitor just cut 12%

Problem

A small distributor carries hundreds of SKUs and has nobody who walks competitors' price lists every day. Prices move quietly.

  • Competitor prices get checked by hand — or not at all.
  • You hear about a price cut from the customer who already compared.
  • The deal goes to whoever is 8% cheaper.
“The data is public. What's missing is the habit of looking at it every day.” — from the product brief

Three steps

How it works

  1. 01

    Add products and links

    Start with 5 products and 3 competitors. Each site gets a profile: where exactly the price lives on the page.

  2. 02

    A snapshot a day

    Collection runs on saved demo pages. A live site plugs in as a separate adapter once the mechanics work.

  3. 03

    See the move, get a signal

    The panel shows change since the last snapshot, history and market spread. Cross the threshold — an alert and a daily digest go out.

Live sites block bots and change their layout, so the demo runs on saved pages. That's an honest limitation, not a bug.

Features

What's inside

  • 01

    Daily snapshot

    Every competitor's price lands in history once a day — no manual spreadsheets.

  • 02

    Change since last time

    See what moved and by how much; yesterday's number never disappears.

  • 03

    Market spread

    Who's cheaper than you and who's dearer — one table per product.

  • 04

    Threshold alerts

    When a competitor drops below what you can accept, a signal and a daily digest arrive.

  • 05

    CSV export

    History goes into your own spreadsheets without copy-paste.

  • 06

    Site profiles

    Selectors live in data, not in code: a new site is a new profile.
